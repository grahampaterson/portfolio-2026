---
term: Path dependence
---

Borrowed from economics. A decision becomes "sticky" not because it's optimal,
but because of the accumulated history of choices built on top of it.

The **QWERTY effect**, or lock-in effect, is a named case of this: a suboptimal
design persists because too many people have already learned it. The cost isn't
in the design — it's in everything that has grown around it.
